# Blind spots

- All 187,242 retained rows have null time_utc; only 1,882 rows contain timestamp-like cues and 31 are invalid calendar expressions.
- Stable IDs are storage identifiers, not identities or principals.
- Redaction removes content, credential values, paths, hostnames, and endpoints from rows.
- Parent links are retained metadata, not execution order.
- Near-body candidate information is statistical candidate evidence, not admission of coordination; the 18,852,564-row candidate matrix is intentionally not retained in the workspace.
- Row order is not time and must not be read as sequence.
- External chronology claims are structural correlations unless they map to a specific row through an explicit precursor in the local notes.
